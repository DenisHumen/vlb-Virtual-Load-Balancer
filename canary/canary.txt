vlb-canary-v1-do-not-edit

This file is fetched by vlb over each configured uplink to verify that the
bytes coming back are genuinely ours.

Why it exists
-------------
An ISP whose account has lapsed usually does not black-hole traffic. It
intercepts it: DNS answers are rewritten to a portal address and HTTP
requests are answered with a payment page. Every reachability check still
passes -- the next hop pings, 1.1.1.1 pings, DNS "replies" -- so a probe that
only asks "do packets come back?" reports the uplink as perfectly healthy
while nothing actually works.

Fetching a file whose contents are known in advance closes that gap. An
interceptor can fake reachability for free; it cannot produce content it does
not have. If the marker on the first line does not come back, the uplink is
not carrying our traffic to the real internet, and vlb fails over.

Contract
--------
The first line is the marker that vlb matches against. Do not edit, move or
rename this file, and do not add anything above the marker: deployments in
the field pin their canary check to it, and changing it makes every one of
them believe their primary uplink has been hijacked.

If you need different canary content, add a new file rather than editing
this one.
